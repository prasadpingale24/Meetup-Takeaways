# AWS re:Invent Vegas - A Recap Session From AWS User Group Pune

## About re:Invent Vegas

AWS re:Invent is the premier annual conference for the global cloud computing community, transforming the Las Vegas Strip into a massive hub for technical education, product launches, and high-level networking. Traditionally attracting over 60,000 attendees, the event features a packed schedule of more than 2,000 sessions ranging from beginner-level overviews to "level 400" deep dives. Beyond the learning, it is famous for its vibrant community atmosphere, including the "Midnight Madness" pep rally and the massive "re:Play" wrap-up party.

The most recent conference, held from **December 1 to December 5, 2025**, was a landmark event that centered on the shift from AI assistants to **autonomous AI agents**. Key highlights included:

- **Frontier Agents:** The launch of **Kiro** (an autonomous engineering agent), alongside specialized agents for Security and DevOps, designed to act as proactive members of software teams.
- **Next-Gen Hardware:** The debut of **Graviton5** processors and **Trainium3 UltraServers**, which use 3nm technology to drastically reduce the cost and energy required for AI training.
- **Amazon Nova:** The expansion of the **Nova model family**, introducing "open training" via Nova Forge to allow companies to build custom frontier models more easily.
- **A "Renaissance" Farewell:** Amazon CTO **Dr. Werner Vogels** delivered a poignant final keynote after 14 years, urging developers to become "Renaissance Developers" by mastering systems thinking and curiosity in an AI-driven world.

### About AWS User Group Pune

The **AWS User Group Pune** is a vibrant, volunteer-driven community that has become a cornerstone of the cloud ecosystem in Maharashtra. Founded with a "for the community, by the community" vision, the group has grown into a massive network of over **20,000 members**, ranging from students and early-career developers to seasoned AWS Heroes and CTOs. They are known for hosting high-quality, free meetups and workshops that deep-dive into everything from core infrastructure like EC2 and S3 to cutting-edge GenAI and DevSecOps practices.

### About Speakers

#### Omshree Butani

**Omshree Butani** is a prominent cloud engineer and tech community leader known for her work as an **AWS Community Builder** and **Women Techmakers Ambassador**. Specializing in FinOps and DevSecOps. She is particularly active in the **AWS User Group Women In Tech India**, where she leads initiatives to empower women in the cloud ecosystem.

#### Toshal Khawale

**Toshal Khawale** is a seasoned tech leader and community champion who currently serves as a core organizer for the **AWS User Group Pune**, with over 21 years of experience in cloud architecture and DevOps leadership, officially recognized as an **AWS Hero**, a prestigious title awarded for his extensive technical contributions and mentorship within the cloud ecosystem.

#### Shubham Londhe

**Shubham Londhe** is a widely recognized tech educator, content creator, and a leading figure within the **AWS User Group Pune** who recently attained the prestigious title of **Developer Advocate at AWS**. Known to his community as "DevOps Wale Bhaiya," he is the founder of **TrainWithShubham**, a platform through which he has mentored over 10,000 learners in DevOps, Cloud, and AI. Within the Pune community, he is a frequent speaker and organizer, well-regarded for his high-energy technical sessions.
